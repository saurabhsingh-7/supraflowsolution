Odoo ERP Implementation

Service · Odoo modules configuration, custom development and third-party integration

Odoo's modular, open-source ERP is a strong fit for manufacturing, inventory-heavy and multi-department businesses that need more depth than Zoho One offers in finance and manufacturing, without the cost of SAP or Oracle.

What we build

  • Core module configuration. Inventory, Manufacturing (MRP), Accounting, Sales and CRM modules configured to your actual operational workflow — not left on defaults.
  • Custom module development. Python/XML development for processes Odoo's standard modules don't cover out of the box.
  • Multi-company and multi-warehouse setup. For businesses operating across multiple legal entities or locations needing consolidated reporting.
  • Third-party integration. Connecting Odoo to e-commerce platforms, payment gateways, shipping providers and other systems you already run.

Odoo vs Zoho — which fits?

Odoo tends to win for manufacturing-heavy operations needing deep MRP and multi-warehouse logistics. Zoho tends to win for sales/CRM-led businesses wanting faster time-to-value and lower total cost. We'll tell you honestly which fits your operation during the free discovery call rather than pushing whichever we'd rather sell.

How we scope it

Process discovery, module selection, fixed-price implementation plan with a written scope document before any commitment.
