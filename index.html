  • Payment category system — assign components by job role automatically

AI-Driven E-Procurement with Vector Embeddings

We integrated vector embedding technology into e-procurement workflows — enabling intelligent vendor matching, real-time fraud detection on pricing anomalies, and an AI negotiation assistant that recommends optimal deal strategies based on historical data.

  • Vendor profiles converted to embeddings for cosine-similarity matching
  • Anomaly detection flags price quotes deviating from historical trends
  • AI negotiation assistant trained on past deal outcomes
  • Vendor ranking by performance, pricing, and reliability scores
  • Fraud prevention: identifies duplicate or shell vendor bids
  • Context-aware discount strategy recommendations

Vector Embeddings in Zoho Desk

We enhanced Zoho Desk with AI-driven vector embeddings that semantically analyze incoming tickets, detect similarity with past issues, auto-link to known problems, and surface resolutions from a maintained error database — dramatically reducing ticket backlog and resolution time.

  • Incoming tickets vectorized and semantically analyzed
  • Similarity search identifies related past tickets
  • Auto-link tickets to existing Problem records
  • Known Error Database integration for instant resolution suggestions
  • Agents notified of recurring patterns proactively
  • Ticket backlog reduction & improved CSAT scores
