Zoho implementation FAQ

Straight answers to what prospects ask before the first call.

How much does a Zoho CRM implementation cost in India?

A focused Zoho CRM implementation typically runs between ₹75,000 and ₹3,00,000 depending on module count, custom Deluge automation, data-migration volume and third-party integrations. Multi-app Zoho One rollouts spanning CRM, Books, Inventory and People are scoped separately. We provide a fixed-price quote after a free discovery call, so you never receive an open-ended hourly bill.

How long does a Zoho implementation take?

A single-module Zoho CRM rollout usually takes two to four weeks. A multi-app Zoho One deployment covering CRM, Books, Inventory and People typically takes six to twelve weeks. Custom Zoho Creator applications such as payroll or manufacturing systems take eight to sixteen weeks depending on business-logic complexity.

Can you migrate our data from Salesforce, HubSpot or spreadsheets into Zoho?

Yes. We handle full migration from Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Freshsales, Tally, QuickBooks and raw Excel or CSV files. Every migration includes field mapping, de-duplication, a sandbox dry run and a post-migration reconciliation report, so you can verify record counts and field integrity before go-live.

Do you build custom payroll in Zoho for countries Zoho Payroll does not support?

Yes. Zoho Payroll is only natively available in select regions. For every other country we build a compliant payroll engine on Zoho Creator that syncs employee masters from Zoho People, applies region-specific tax and deduction tables through Deluge map structures, and posts validated double-entry journals into Zoho Books via API.

What is Deluge scripting and why does it matter?

Deluge is Zoho's proprietary scripting language used across CRM, Creator, Desk, Books and Flow. It is what turns a stock Zoho setup into a system that matches your actual process — custom validation, cross-app record sync, API integrations and scheduled jobs. Most failed Zoho projects fail because nobody wrote proper Deluge. A certified Deluge developer is the difference between owning a licence and owning a working system.

Do you provide support after the Zoho project goes live?

Yes. Every engagement includes thirty days of post-go-live hypercare covering bug fixes, user questions and workflow tuning at no extra cost. After that we offer monthly retainers covering admin support, new automation, user training and quarterly system health reviews.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We deliver remotely to clients across the UAE, United Kingdom, United States, Singapore and Australia, with overlapping working hours arranged per region. Our team is based in Noida, India and works Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST.

Can you integrate Zoho with AI tools like ChatGPT or voice agents?

Yes. We build AI layers on top of Zoho including vector-embedding search for Zoho Desk ticket deduplication, LLM-driven lead scoring inside Zoho CRM, and autonomous outbound voice agents that qualify leads and write transcripts plus status back into CRM records.
