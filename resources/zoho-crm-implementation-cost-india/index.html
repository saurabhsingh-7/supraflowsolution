Zoho CRM Implementation Cost in India (2026)

Updated 24 September 2026 · By the Supraflow Solutions consulting team · 9 min read

Short answer: Zoho CRM licences in India cost ₹800 to ₹2,600 per user per month on annual billing, before 18% GST. Implementation by a partner is a separate one-time fee, typically ₹75,000 to ₹3,00,000 for a small or mid-sized business, and ₹3,00,000 to ₹8,00,000+ for enterprise rollouts with custom modules and multiple integrations.

A realistic first-year budget for a 10-user team on Professional is ₹1,68,000 in licences + ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,00,000 in implementation — roughly ₹2.7 to ₹3.7 lakh before GST.

Most pricing pages stop at the licence cost. That is the smaller and more predictable half of the number. The part that actually decides whether the project succeeds — and what it really costs — is the implementation, and almost nobody publishes rates for it.

This page breaks down both, with the working shown.

Monthly billing costs roughly 20% more than annual. GST at 18% is added on top, and is recoverable as input tax credit if you are GST-registered — so the effective cost stays close to the listed rate.

The edition mistake that costs the most

Enterprise gets sold on Zia AI and territory management. If you have one sales team, one price list and no discount approval chain, Professional does the same job at ₹1,000 per user per month less. On a ten-seat team that is ₹1,20,000 a year — more than the entire implementation fee for many projects.

Move to Enterprise when territories, sandbox testing or custom modules become an actual requirement, not a wish. You can upgrade at any time; you rarely get a refund for downgrading.

2. What implementation actually costs

Partner fees are a one-time project cost, separate from licences. The range is wide because the work is genuinely different between a five-user pipeline and a multi-team rollout with ERP integration.

4. The costs nobody quotes you

  • 18% GST on everything. A ₹2,00,000 implementation invoices at ₹2,36,000. Always check whether a quote is inclusive or exclusive before comparing two proposals.
  • Monthly billing penalty. Paying monthly instead of annually costs roughly 20% more, every year, forever.
  • Add-on apps. Zoho Desk, Campaigns, SalesIQ and Analytics each carry their own licence unless you bundle into Zoho One or CRM Plus.
  • Extra storage. Roughly ₹4 per GB per month past your plan limit. Small, but it appears.
  • New hires. Every additional user adds to the annual bill. Budget for headcount, not just today's team.
  • Your own team's time. Someone internally has to answer questions and run UAT. Real cost, no invoice.
  • Rework. The most expensive line of all. A cheap setup done badly gets rebuilt in eight months and you pay twice.

5. How to keep the number sensible

  1. Start on Standard or Professional. Upgrade when a specific feature becomes a blocker, not because Enterprise sounds more serious.
  2. Choose annual billing. It saves about 20% immediately.
  3. Insist on fixed-price scope. Open-ended hourly billing is how a ₹60,000 project becomes ₹2,00,000. Ask for a written scope document and a capped number.
  4. Get integrations quoted as separate line items, each with its own estimate, so you can defer or cap the ones that are not urgent.
  5. Do not cut training. A CRM nobody knows how to use has zero return, regardless of how well it was built.
  6. Phase the rollout. Sales first, then service or finance. Phased projects deliver value faster than big-bang launches and surface problems while they are still cheap to fix.

6. Questions worth asking any partner

Before signing anything, ask these. The answers separate consultants from resellers:

  • "Is this a fixed price for an agreed scope, or time and material?" — if T&M, ask what the ceiling is.
  • "What exactly is in the scope document, line by line?"
  • "Who does the work — the person I am speaking to, or someone else after signing?"
  • "What happens in the 30 days after go-live, and is it charged?"
  • "Show me a system you built for a business like mine."
  • "What will this cost me in year two?"

Buying licences through a partner costs the same as buying direct from Zoho — the partner margin comes from Zoho, not from a markup on your licence. If a partner quotes above Zoho's published rate, ask why.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Zoho CRM cost per user in India?

On annual billing, excluding 18% GST: Standard ₹800, Professional ₹1,400, Enterprise ₹2,400 and Ultimate ₹2,600 per user per month. A free edition supports up to 3 users. Monthly billing costs roughly 20% more per user than annual.

Is implementation a one-time cost or recurring?

Implementation is a one-time project fee. Licences renew annually and optional support retainers are monthly. From year two you mainly pay licences plus whatever support you choose, so the total cost drops sharply after the first year.

Does buying Zoho through a partner cost more?

No. Zoho sets licence pricing and a partner cannot mark it up — the partner's margin comes from Zoho. You pay the same published rate buying through a partner as buying direct, and you gain implementation support. Implementation services are quoted separately.

How long does a Zoho CRM implementation take?

A clean Standard rollout for a small team takes two to four weeks. A Professional setup with automation and one or two integrations takes four to eight weeks. Enterprise projects with custom modules, approval chains and ERP integration run eight to sixteen weeks.

Can we implement Zoho CRM ourselves and skip the partner fee?

Yes, and for a very small team with a simple pipeline that is often the right call — Zoho is genuinely usable out of the box. Bring in a partner when you need enforced process (Blueprint), custom Deluge automation, data migration from another CRM, or integrations with accounting, telephony or WhatsApp. The honest test: if you cannot describe your sales process in writing, no partner can configure it for you either. Start there first.

Is GST charged on Zoho licences and implementation?

Yes, 18% on both. If your business is GST-registered, that component is recoverable as input tax credit, so the effective cost stays close to the listed price. Always confirm whether a quote is GST-inclusive or exclusive before comparing proposals.

What is the cheapest way to run Zoho CRM in India?

Free edition for up to 3 users, or Standard at ₹800 per user per month on annual billing. If you need several Zoho apps — CRM plus Books, Desk or Projects — compare against Zoho One, which often works out cheaper than buying three or four apps separately.
