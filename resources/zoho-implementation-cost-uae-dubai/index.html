Zoho Implementation Cost in UAE & Dubai (2026 Guide)

Updated 23 October 2026 · By the Supraflow Solutions consulting team · 7 min read

Short answer: Zoho CRM licences in the UAE run roughly AED 75 to AED 240 per user per month on annual billing, before 5% VAT. Implementation by a partner is quoted separately as a fixed project fee, typically AED 4,000 to AED 15,000 for a small or mid-sized business, and AED 15,000 to AED 40,000+ for enterprise rollouts with custom modules, multi-branch architecture and ERP integration.

We implement Zoho for clients across India, the UAE, the UK and the US. The UAE market has its own pricing reality — VAT treatment, AED billing, and local payment gateway integrations (Tabby, Tamara, Network International) that generic India-focused pricing pages don't cover. This page does.

1. Zoho CRM licence cost in the UAE

Zoho bills globally in USD, converted to AED at the prevailing rate on your invoice. These are approximate per-user, per-month figures on annual billing, excluding 5% VAT.

4. UAE-specific factors generic pricing pages miss

  • 5% VAT. Applies to licences and services. VAT-registered businesses can usually reclaim it as input tax.
  • Free zone vs mainland. Invoice numbering, VAT treatment and compliance fields need configuring to your actual registration — not a generic default.
  • Bilingual requirements. Arabic/English invoice and quote templates are common client requirements, and not every partner builds these correctly the first time.
  • Local payment gateways. Tabby, Tamara and Network International are UAE-specific integrations most India-only consultants haven't configured before.
  • Multi-branch standardisation. Many UAE businesses also operate an India or wider-GCC branch — replicating one governed architecture across locations is its own scoped work, not an afterthought.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Zoho CRM cost per user in the UAE?

Zoho prices in USD globally and the UAE is billed in AED at the prevailing rate. Roughly: Standard ~AED 75/user/month, Professional ~AED 130/user/month, Enterprise ~AED 220/user/month, Ultimate ~AED 240/user/month on annual billing, before 5% VAT.

Is 5% UAE VAT charged on Zoho subscriptions and implementation?

Yes. UAE VAT at 5% applies to both Zoho licence fees and local implementation services. VAT-registered businesses can typically reclaim it as input tax, keeping the effective cost close to the listed price.

Can a free zone company use Zoho the same way as a mainland company?

Yes, Zoho's cloud products work identically for free zone and mainland UAE entities. The difference is in configuration — VAT treatment, invoice numbering, and compliance fields should reflect your specific free zone or mainland registration during setup, not generic defaults.

Do I need a local UAE partner or can an India-based team implement Zoho remotely?

Zoho implementation is entirely remote-capable — configuration, Deluge scripting and training all happen over screen-share. What matters is the team's familiarity with UAE-specific requirements (VAT, Arabic/English bilingual templates, local payment gateways like Tabby and Telr) rather than physical location.
