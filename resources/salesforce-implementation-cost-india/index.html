Salesforce Implementation Cost in India (2026)

Updated 28 October 2026 · By the Supraflow Solutions consulting team · 9 min read

Short answer: Salesforce Sales Cloud is priced in US dollars even on its India pricing page: Starter Suite $25, Pro Suite $100, Core $195, Advanced $395, Max $550 per user per month, billed annually (Starter can be monthly), plus 18% GST on the converted rupee invoice. Implementation by a partner is a separate one-time fee, typically ₹1,50,000 to ₹5,00,000 for a small or mid-sized rollout, and ₹8,00,000 to ₹25,00,000+ for enterprise projects with custom Apex development and multiple integrations.

A realistic first-year budget for a 10-user team on Pro Suite is roughly ₹11.4 lakh in licences (at ~₹95/USD) plus ₹2,00,000 to ₹4,00,000 in implementation, before GST.

Salesforce does not publish a rupee price for India the way Zoho does. Every tier on salesforce.com/in is quoted in USD, which means the real number an Indian business pays depends on the exchange rate on invoice day, not a fixed list price. That single fact trips up more budgets than any implementation line item.

This page works through both the licence side and the implementation side, with the currency conversion made explicit rather than glossed over.

The edition mistake that costs the most

Core gets sold on Slack and Tableau integration. If your sales team has one pipeline and no need for Slack-native deal rooms, Pro Suite does the equivalent CRM job for roughly $95 per user per month less. On a ten-seat team that is close to $11,400 a year, more than most implementation budgets for a project that size. Move up only when a specific Core or Advanced feature is an actual blocker, not a nice-to-have on a slide deck.

2. What Salesforce implementation actually costs

Salesforce implementations tend to run higher than Zoho implementations for the same company size, mainly because Salesforce's deeper customisation model (Apex code, Flow Builder, a much larger object and permission model) takes more specialist hours to configure correctly, and certified Salesforce consultants bill at a premium over Zoho/Deluge specialists in the Indian market.

Enterprise projects with Apex development, multiple integrations and complex approval hierarchies run ₹8,00,000 to ₹25,00,000 and above.

4. The costs nobody quotes you

  • Exchange rate drift. A multi-year contract quoted in USD moves with the rupee. Ask whether your renewal is rate-locked or re-priced annually.
  • 18% GST on everything, calculated on the converted INR amount, which itself moves with the exchange rate.
  • AppExchange add-ons. Many useful packages (CPQ tools, document generation, advanced reporting) are licensed separately per user, same pattern as Zoho Marketplace but at steeper AppExchange price points.
  • A permanent admin need. Salesforce's flexibility means almost every org ends up needing a part-time or full-time admin, which Zoho setups more often avoid.
  • Sandbox and storage limits. Extra sandboxes and data storage beyond plan limits are billed add-ons, more aggressively tiered than Zoho's per-GB overage.
  • Premier Support. Faster support SLAs are an add-on even on paid editions, not included by default below Advanced/Max.
  • Rework from over-customisation. Salesforce's power makes it easy to over-build. A process that should take two weeks to configure can balloon to two months if scope is not capped up front.

5. How to keep the number sensible

  1. Start on Starter or Pro Suite. Upgrade to Core or Advanced only when a named feature becomes a genuine blocker.
  2. Ask for a rate-locked or INR-denominated contract if your finance team needs budget certainty, not every Salesforce reseller offers this, but it is worth asking.
  3. Insist on fixed-price scope for implementation. Apex and Flow work is the easiest category to let run open-ended on time and material billing.
  4. Get each integration quoted as a separate line item so you can defer the ones that are not launch-critical.
  5. Budget for an ongoing admin from day one, in-house or retained, rather than discovering the need three months after go-live.
  6. Compare against Zoho CRM honestly before committing, if your sales process does not need Apex-level customisation or the Salesforce ecosystem specifically, the total cost of ownership gap over three years is substantial.

6. Questions worth asking any partner

Before signing anything, ask these. The answers separate consultants from resellers:

  • "Is this fixed price for an agreed scope, or time and material?", if T&M, ask what the ceiling is.
  • "What exchange rate are you using to quote this in rupees, and does it get re-checked at renewal?"
  • "What exactly is in the scope document, object by object, Flow by Flow?"
  • "Who writes the Apex, a certified developer, or a junior admin learning on my org?"
  • "What ongoing admin support do I need after go-live, and is it included?"
  • "Show me a Salesforce org you built for a business at my scale."

Salesforce AppExchange partners cannot mark up licence pricing either, Salesforce sets it, and partner margin comes from implementation and support services, same principle as Zoho.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Salesforce Sales Cloud cost per user in India?

Salesforce prices Sales Cloud in US dollars even on its India pricing page, not in rupees. As of late 2026 the published tiers are Starter Suite $25, Pro Suite $100, Core $195, Advanced $395 and Max $550 per user per month, billed annually (Starter can be billed monthly). At a rough ₹95/USD rate that is roughly ₹2,400 to ₹52,000 per user per month before 18% GST, and moves with the exchange rate on your invoice date.

Does Salesforce publish INR pricing for India?

No. Unlike Zoho, Salesforce's India pricing page lists every Sales Cloud edition in USD only. Your rupee cost is the USD list price multiplied by the exchange rate on your billing date, plus 18% GST on the Indian invoice. Get a written INR quote from your account executive or partner before budgeting rather than estimating off a single day's rate.

Is Salesforce implementation a one-time cost or recurring?

Implementation is a one-time project fee, same as with Zoho. Licences renew annually (or you can be billed monthly on Starter Suite) and any admin retainer or support contract is a recurring monthly cost. The implementation line drops out entirely from year two onward unless you commission new work.

How long does a Salesforce implementation take?

A lean Starter or Pro Suite rollout for a small sales team takes three to six weeks. A Core or Advanced implementation with validation rules, approval processes and one or two integrations runs six to twelve weeks. Enterprise-scale projects with custom objects, Apex development and ERP integration commonly run three to six months.

Why does Salesforce cost so much more than Zoho in India?

Mainly currency and edition design. Salesforce prices in USD with no India-specific discount, so the rupee cost scales with the exchange rate, and even its entry Starter Suite at $25/user/month works out close to what Zoho charges for its top Enterprise tier. Salesforce's deeper customisation (Apex, Flow, a far larger AppExchange and partner ecosystem) is real, but most Indian SMBs and mid-market teams do not need that depth and end up paying for headroom they will not use.

Can a small Indian business realistically afford Salesforce?

Yes, on Starter Suite or Pro Suite for a small team, though the honest comparison still favours Zoho CRM for a sales process that does not need Apex-level customisation. Salesforce becomes the better fit once you need its specific ecosystem: a large AppExchange install base, Slack-native deal rooms, or a global rollout where Salesforce is already the standard elsewhere in the business.

Is GST charged on Salesforce licences and implementation in India?

Yes, 18% on both the licence invoice and any implementation services billed by an Indian partner, recoverable as input tax credit if your business is GST-registered. Because licences are billed in USD, confirm whether the GST is calculated on the converted INR amount at invoice time, and ask for that conversion in writing.
